The Twelve Reports · 2

Two yardsticks

At Hebron the twelve saw the sons of Anak and a walled city. All twelve agreed about that. What they measured it against is where they split.

Just the facts: a giant, a wall, a gate. The measuring rod shows how tall he is.

Watch the measuring rod. The giant is the same height in both reports. Only the yardstick changed.

Case JM-13 · The Twelve Reports

Same facts, two reports

Twelve honest men walked one road for forty days and came back with one bunch of grapes and two reports. Ten said “we are not able”. Two said “let us go up at once”.

Work through the three tables. As you go, keep asking: is this something they saw, or something they concluded?

All three tables worked

What separates the reports

Not one fact. Every observation was in both reports. The ten measured the giants against themselves; the two measured them against the LORD. The giants did not change size.

“Only don’t rebel against the LORD, neither fear the people of the land; for they are bread for us. Their defence is removed from over them, and the LORD is with us. Don’t fear them.”Numbers 14:9
