# CASE JM-00 — The Maker's Mark · Image prompts

Ten images: the shelf poster, six plates for the documents, and three evidence
photographs. Send them back named as listed and I'll wire them in. The case
already works without them: the poster is a plain starfield for now, and the
documents are text only.

## Rules for this case

- **No words in any image.** No labels, captions, letters or numbers; I add
  those as real text afterwards, as with every other case.
- **No faces of real people.** Hubble, Lemaître, Hoyle and the other scientists
  are named in the documents but never pictured. Show their instruments and
  desks, not them.
- **No face of God, no hand from the sky, no light-beam "creation" moments.**
  The case argues from the work to the Maker, so the pictures show only the
  work.
- **Accurate nature.** Monarchs are eastern monarchs (orange with black veins
  and white dots); the Mexican forests are oyamel firs; honeybees are honeybees.

## Style blocks

**Plates** (documents, 1536×1024 unless stated). Paste after each plate prompt:

> Detailed vintage field-guide illustration, ink linework with soft
> watercolour wash on aged cream paper, muted natural palette with deep blue
> and gold accents, gentle lighting, a small blank cream label plaque in one
> corner with nothing written on it. No text, no letters, no numbers, no
> watermark.

**Evidence photographs** (1536×1024). Paste after each evidence prompt:

> Forensic evidence photograph, close-up, hard flash, sharp focus, crisp
> shadows, slightly cold colour. Small blank yellow folding evidence markers
> stand beside the objects, with nothing printed on them. A plain grey
> photographic scale bar with no numbers lies along the bottom edge. No text,
> no numbers, no watermark.
